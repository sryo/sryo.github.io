I have a bad habit of leaving apps open: Slack minimized, Figma in the background, three Finder windows I forgot about. By afternoon my Mac is sluggish, so I built [AppTidy](https://github.com/sryo/AppTidy) to do the cleanup quietly from the menu bar.

## What it does

- Quits apps that have had no open windows for a set time: 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes (5 by default)
- Leaves alone anything on the Keep Alive list and, by default, anything playing audio
- After any quit or closed Finder window, a toast offers undo: ⌘⌥Z within 5 seconds relaunches the app or reopens the folder
- Lives in the menu bar with no Dock icon; the timeout, undo period and shortcut are in its preferences

## How it works

DIAGRAM: diagram.svg "Every quit, automatic or by hand, leaves a few seconds to take it back"

Every 5 seconds it asks the Accessibility API how many windows each app has, and notes when the count drops to zero. Past the timeout it checks Core Audio and Now Playing, then quits, forcing it after 2 seconds if the app hangs on. A workspace notification catches every quit and holds an undo token for the toast.

## Details

- Swift with AppKit and SwiftUI, for macOS 13 and later
- Needs Accessibility permission to count windows; without it the scan skips rather than guessing
- The ⌘⌥Z shortcut is a global Carbon hotkey, re-recordable in preferences
- A tagged push builds a Developer ID signed, notarized zip on GitHub Actions
