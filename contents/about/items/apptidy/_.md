I have a bad habit of leaving apps open: Slack minimized, Figma in the background, three Finder windows I forgot about. By afternoon my Mac is sluggish, so I built [AppTidy](https://github.com/sryo/AppTidy) to do the cleanup quietly from the menu bar.

## What it does

- Quits apps that have had no open windows for a set time: 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes (5 by default)
- Leaves alone anything on the Keep Alive list and, by default, anything playing audio
- After any quit or closed Finder window, a toast gives you 5 seconds to undo: the app relaunches or the folder reopens

## Decisions

### Undo, not a question

AppTidy never asks before it quits something, because asking every time would annoy you. It quits, then shows a small toast with the app's name and a countdown. Undo within 5 seconds and the app comes back. The same toast covers apps you quit yourself and Finder windows you close.

### The music keeps playing

A music player with its window closed looks forgotten, but it isn't. By default AppTidy skips any app that's playing audio, so the music never stops.
