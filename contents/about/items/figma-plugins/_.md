Two Figma plugins, [Anchorify](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1284214046492867185/anchorify) and [Word Hugger](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1285425464785405153/word-hugger), from one hack day, both fixing things that bugged me about other plugins. Google Sheets Sync was the closest thing I'd found, and it stops short of making links clickable.

## What it does

- Anchorify turns `&#91;text&#93;(url)`, `&lt;a href="url"&gt;` and `[url=url]text[/url]` markup into clickable, underlined hyperlinks.
- It also links bare URLs and email addresses. A link aimed at a frame, as in `&#91;See pricing&#93;(-&gt; Pricing)`, jumps to the frame named Pricing instead of a URL.
- Word Hugger **fixes orphaned words** (those lonely single words on a line) by gluing the last two words of each paragraph together. Because every word deserves company.

## How it works

DIAGRAM: diagram.svg "Both plugins rewrite text layers in place, then tell you how many they changed"

Each plugin is one `code.js` with no UI. It takes the selected text layers, or every one on the page, loads their fonts and edits the characters directly. Anchorify swaps markup for its link text; Word Hugger swaps the last space for a non-breaking one and restores each paragraph's styles.

## Details

- Figma Plugin API, plain JavaScript, no UI and no network access
- Published on Figma Community; Word Hugger adds a relaunch button to the page
- Source: [Anchorify](https://github.com/sryo/Anchorify) and [Word Hugger](https://github.com/sryo/Word-Hugger) on GitHub
- Anchorify edits from the last match back, so earlier positions stay valid
