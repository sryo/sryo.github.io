Every budgeting app I've tried dies the same death: I stop entering expenses. The friction of typing amounts kills the habit within a week. [Fin](https://github.com/sryo/Fin) skips the typing.

## What it does

- Point the camera at a receipt, or pick a PDF, and Fin fills in the total, merchant and date.
- It suggests a category from where you shopped. Confirm and done; typing it in by hand still works.
- The spending view is a treemap: bigger rectangles mean more money spent in that category. No line graphs, no dashboards, just "where did my money go this month?"

## How it works

DIAGRAM: diagram.svg "OCR plus regex and the Donut model each read the receipt; the best of both prefills the form"

Vision reads the text and a regex parser picks out amounts, dates and known merchants. Donut, an encoder-decoder vision model running in Core ML, reads the image itself. Fin merges them, trusting regex when it's sure, then matches category keywords.

## Details

- Swift and SwiftUI for iOS 17, SwiftData storage, no external dependencies
- Donut runs fully on device with a 15-second cap; the regex parser covers when it fails
- Nine default categories, suggested by keyword lists of Argentine merchants like Coto, YPF and Edenor
- Amounts and dates formatted for Argentine Spanish (es_AR)
- Squarified treemap layout, drawn in SwiftUI
