Every budgeting app I've tried dies the same death: I stop entering expenses. The friction of typing amounts kills the habit within a week. [Fin](https://github.com/sryo/Fin) skips the typing.

## What it does

- Point the camera at a receipt, or pick a PDF, and Fin fills in the total, merchant and date.
- It suggests a category from where you shopped. Confirm and done; typing it in by hand still works.
- The spending view is a treemap: bigger rectangles mean more money spent in that category. No line graphs, no dashboards, just "where did my money go this month?"

## Decisions

### A photo instead of typing

Typing amounts is what made me quit every other budgeting app. So a photo of the receipt fills in the form. The amount field is still there, ready, for when typing is quicker.

### A treemap, not line graphs

I chose a treemap over line graphs: it's the best use of the screen. The home screen answers one question: where did my money go this month? Each category is a block sized by what you spent there. Tap one to see what's inside, or scroll the month at the top to look back.

### It suggests, you confirm

Fin guesses the category from the store, but it never files anything on its own. The guess shows up selected, and you save it or tap another. A scan only fills the fields you left empty, so it never overwrites what you typed. It's a test of data entry where a person confirms or corrects every guess.
