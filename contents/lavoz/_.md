## Context

Online readership was growing, but print advertising had dropped by 40%, and digital had to become the primary revenue source against an audience that expected news to be free.

Other papers were hesitant to add paywalls. We tried anyway.

## My role

From August 2017 to February 2019 I was UX Researcher and Product Designer at *La Voz del Interior*, Córdoba's 115-year-old paper, and led its digital strategy: convincing readers to pay for news online while print revenue fell.

I managed 3 UX designers and developers through the AMP redesign, writing the AMP, Sass, PHP and JS alongside them.

## Decisions

### We built our own paywall, Wyleex

The core components:
- Metering logic that tracks article consumption per user
- Content tiers separating free, premium, and exclusive stories
- Subscription management integrated with local payment processors

Building in-house meant we controlled the pricing model. Licensed paywalls were priced in dollars; ours let us offer peso-denominated subscriptions, keep revenue sustainable through currency swings, and adjust tiers based on what Argentine readers would actually pay.

DIAGRAM: wyleex.svg "One paywall we owned end to end: metered, tiered and priced in pesos"

### Mobile-first storytelling

Younger readers were on Instagram and WhatsApp, not reading long broadsheets. We had to meet them where they were.
- **Visual storytelling:** I shifted the newsroom from text-heavy articles to photo, video and infographic formats built for the thumb-scroll.
- **Clean design:** We rolled out a minimalist interface that put content first, removing the clutter that drove mobile users away.
- **AMP and a PWA:** The mobile-first AMP redesign relaunched with zero downtime, and I built the progressive web app.
- **Web Stories:** We shipped the visual format, and it lifted pageviews and time on site.

DIAGRAM: mobile.svg "Stories told in pictures, delivered to the phone three ways"

I also prototyped an AI visual-storytelling tool: an NLP pipeline detects the entities in a journalist's text and suggests a best-fit image for sharing, and the writer approves it before anything is published.

## Outcome

In February 2018, the paywall went live.

METRICS:
- **10K** subscribers in 6 months
- **25K** subscribers in 2 years
- **Finalist** WAN-IFRA Digital Media LATAM 2019

This project required convincing a newsroom steeped in tradition to tell stories differently. The paywall worked. We later offered Wyleex as a service to other publishers in Latin America.
