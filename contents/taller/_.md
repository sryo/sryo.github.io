## Context

Taller Technologies is a U.S. software engineering company with a delivery center in Córdoba, Argentina. I worked there from November 2009 to October 2016, designing for embedded hardware (digital TV, a farm-cab console, drones and satellite missions) alongside web work for U.S. clients.

## My role

UX/UI Designer. I designed interactive TV apps and their companion app, the precision-agriculture console, and UIs for drones, satellite missions, Trint, EnerSys and ASSIA's CloudCheck broadband diagnostics. I also built the front-end for PeerAssist's payments flow and led internal HTML5/CSS3 training.

## Decisions

### Interactive apps for digital TV

TDA is Argentina's digital terrestrial TV. I designed interactive apps for it, written in Lua on the Ginga middleware. A companion app helps people locate and orient their home antenna.

DIAGRAM: tv.svg "The apps run on Ginga; the companion app helps aim the antenna"

### An in-cabin console for precision agriculture

I designed the embedded in-cabin console for Abelardo Cuffia's GPS/RTK precision-agriculture platform: autosteer, guidance, section control, variable-rate application and seed monitoring.

DIAGRAM: console.svg "An RTK position feeds the in-cabin console and the functions it covers"

### Leading web publishing on Agilent

On Agilent, a global life-sciences company, over a 5+ year engagement, I led 20 designers as regional lead for web publishing: QA, progress reporting, templates, layouts, and internal HTML5/CSS3 and UX training.
